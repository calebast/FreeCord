# Code of Conduct

FreeCord contributors are expected to make participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward others. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participation when conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
